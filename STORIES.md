almost 500,000 deaths in war in Ukraine." The page prints the figure

**2 — MIDDLE EAST · Coalition bombs Hodeidah; Jeddah named.**
Reuters, Wed 25 Nov: Yemen's Houthis said on Wednesday that Jeddah
and all other Saudi ports on the Red Sea coast would be targeted if
Saudi Arabia attacked Hodeidah; after the statement, the Saudi-led
coalition targeted operational capabilities at Hodeidah port and
destroyed targets in Saada and Amran (coalition statement, Wed),
following a Tuesday-evening evacuation warning. The Tuesday drone
strike on a Riyadh financial tower (11 injured) is context only and
is named in the date-check box, not counted as a story.

**3 — ASIA · A Pakistani rides Shenzhou-24 into orbit.**
BBC News, Wed 25 Nov 11:28 ("Moment Chinese space mission lifts off
with first Pakistan astronaut aboard"; article the same day):
Muhammad Zeeshan Ali, a former fighter pilot, is Pakistan's first
astronaut and the first foreigner to join a Chinese crewed mission;
Shenzhou-24 launched from Jiuquan. Euronews carried the launch the
same day.

**7 — ASIA · Rescuers hunt for 26 trapped miners.** Reuters,

West Java police put the trapped at 26 (11 escaped on Monday when
the Ciseureuh River flooded the shafts). Note: AP and Xinhua said
